Popayán, Colombia — June 2026

To the Selection Committee
SUCCESS Project — Global Distributed Software Development (GDSD) Course
Universidad del Cauca

Dear Committee,

My name is Yeison Delgado, and I am writing to express my genuine interest in joining the GDSD course.
I am an engineering student who builds AI software in practice, not only in theory: I have worked on
production systems such as real-time voice agents, RAG-based assistants and multi-agent workflows. That
experience taught me something this course promises to deepen: shipping real software is as much about
people, communication and process as it is about code.

What draws me to GDSD is the chance to work the way global technology companies actually work. Solving a
real challenge for an industry client, inside an international team spread across Sweden, Finland, Spain,
Panama, Montenegro and Colombia, is exactly the environment I want to grow in. I am used to remote,
asynchronous collaboration, and I am eager to push myself further: coordinating across time zones,
navigating cultural differences, and delivering under agile (Scrum) practices with real accountability.

I believe I can contribute and learn at the same time. I can bring hands-on experience with AI,
backend engineering and production-minded thinking, while learning structured global delivery, European
best practices and the discipline of working with mentors from both academia and industry. I work well
in teams, I communicate clearly, and I take ownership of what I commit to.

Beyond the technical side, I see this course as a bridge between the academic world and the industry I
want to build my career in. The network of peers, mentors and partner companies is, for me, as valuable
as the project itself. I am ready to commit the time, the energy and the responsibility this experience
demands.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be honored to represent Universidad del Cauca in this
international team and to give my best to the client and to my teammates.

Sincerely,

Yeison Estiven Delgado Ordoñez
Universidad del Cauca — yeisondelgado@unicauca.edu.co
