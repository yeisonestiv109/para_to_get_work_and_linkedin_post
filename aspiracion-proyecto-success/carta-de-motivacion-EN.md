Dear Committee,

My name is Yeison Delgado. I am an engineering student, but I am also already working in the field I want
to build my career in: for the past 14+ months I have been a Lead AI Engineer at Glovar Services,
delivering production software entirely remotely. I am applying to GDSD because I want to take the
experience I already have and stretch it into a truly global, distributed setting.

I do not build slideware; I ship systems. At Glovar I designed and deployed a production B2B multi-agent
platform on autoscaling serverless infrastructure, and I built a real-time voice agent with sub-second
end-to-end latency using Twilio, Deepgram and an LLM. In a separate challenge with 50 competing teams, my
retail AI agent placed in the top 5; there I implemented a hybrid RAG architecture and a strict
"zero-hallucination" policy where the agent never answers without auditable evidence. In parallel, my Edge
AI research reached an F1-Macro of 0.998 with ~93.8 ms inference on a low-power device. I share these not
to boast, but to be honest about the level I can contribute from day one.

What I am missing, and what GDSD offers, is the international and distributed dimension done properly.
I already work remotely and asynchronously, with Git, iterative delivery and CI/CD; now I want to do it
inside a multicultural team spanning Sweden, Finland, Spain, Panama, Montenegro and Colombia, under agile
(Scrum) practices, coordinating across time zones for a real industry client. Learning European best
practices, working with academic and industry mentors, and being accountable to an actual customer is
exactly the kind of growth I am looking for.

I am confident I can give as much as I take. I can contribute hands-on AI and backend engineering, a
production-minded approach to reliability, latency and cost, and a teammate who communicates clearly and
owns his commitments. In return, I want to become sharper at structured global delivery and cross-cultural
collaboration, which I see as essential for the rest of my career.

I am ready to commit the time, the energy and the responsibility this course requires. It would be an
honor to represent Universidad del Cauca in this international team and to give my best to both the client
and my teammates.

Sincerely,
